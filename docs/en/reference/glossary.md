# Glossary

Simulation, statistics and project-evaluation terms used in OpenRiskSim, with their Spanish equivalent in parentheses (as shown in the Spanish interface).

**AIC / BIC** (Akaike / Bayesian information criterion). Criteria to compare models fitted to the same data, combining goodness of fit with a penalty for the number of parameters. Lower is better.

**Assumption** (*supuesto*). Uncertain input cell modelled with a probability distribution.

**Bootstrap**. Technique that resamples data with replacement to estimate a statistic's precision without assuming a distribution.

**Certainty** (*certeza*). Probability, in %, that a forecast falls within an interval. Two-tail, left-tail or right-tail.

**Coefficient of variation (CV)** (*coeficiente de variación*). Standard deviation divided by the absolute mean. Measures relative risk.

**Constraint** (*restricción*). Condition an optimization solution must meet (e.g. budget ≤ 100,000).

**Cumulative distribution (CDF)** (*distribución acumulada*). F(x) = P(X ≤ x). In the **Cumulative** results view you read the probability of not exceeding a value directly.

**Decision variable** (*variable de decisión*). A cell controlled by the decision-maker that the optimizer may change within a range.

**Density (PDF)** (*densidad*). For continuous variables, the function whose area between two values is the probability of falling between them. A simulation histogram approximates the density.

**EAA** (equivalent annual annuity, *VAE*). NPV expressed as a level annuity over the project's life.

**Efficient frontier** (*frontera eficiente*). Set of optimal solutions as a constraint's limit varies — typically maximum return for each level of risk.

**Equity cash flow** (*flujo de caja del inversionista*). Project cash flow after financing: loan proceeds, interest, tax shield and principal repayment. Discounted at the cost of equity (Ke).

**Excess kurtosis** (*curtosis*). How heavy the tails are relative to a Normal (0). Positive: more extreme values.

**Forecast** (*pronóstico*). (1) In the simulation model, an output cell whose values are recorded in each trial (NPV, IRR). (2) In the Forecasting tab, projecting a time series into the future.

**Free cash flow / project cash flow** (*flujo de caja del proyecto*). Cash generated by the project regardless of financing. Discounted at the WACC or hurdle rate.

**Hurdle rate** (*TMAR*). Minimum return required by the investor; used as the discount rate.

**Hypothesis test** (*prueba de hipótesis*). Statistical procedure to decide whether an observed difference (e.g. between two means) could be due to chance. The null hypothesis is rejected if the **p-value** is below the significance level α.

**Iman–Conover**. Method to induce rank correlations between simulated variables by reordering their samples, without changing their distributions.

**IRR** (internal rate of return, *TIR*). Discount rate that makes the NPV zero. **MIRR** (*TIRM*): modified version assuming reinvestment at an explicit rate.

**Latin hypercube (LHS)** (*hipercubo latino*). Sampling method that splits each distribution into equal-probability strata and takes one value from each. Converges faster than plain Monte Carlo.

**Mean** (*media*). Average of the results; the **expected value**.

**Mean confidence interval** (*IC de la media*). Range that, with the stated confidence (95%), contains the forecast's true mean. Measures the simulation's precision, not the project's risk.

**Median** (*mediana*). Central value: half the trials are below and half above.

**Monte Carlo**. Method that estimates a model's behaviour by repeating many scenarios with random values.

**NPV** (net present value, *VAN*). Sum of cash flows discounted at the required rate, including the initial investment. NPV > 0: the project creates value.

**Payback period** (*periodo de recuperación, PRI*). Time needed for the cumulative cash flow to recover the investment. **Discounted payback** uses discounted flows.

**Percentile** (*percentil*). The p-th percentile is the value below which p% of results fall. P50 = median.

**Prediction interval** (*intervalo de predicción*). In time-series forecasting, the range where a future value is expected to fall with a given probability (80%, 95%).

**Profitability index (PI, B/C)** (*índice de rentabilidad*). Present value of future flows divided by the initial investment. PI > 1 is equivalent to NPV > 0.

**p-value** (*p-valor*). Probability of a result as extreme as the observed one if the null hypothesis were true. In goodness-of-fit tests, a high p-value means no evidence against the distribution.

**Rank correlation (Spearman)** (*correlación de rangos*). Correlation between the ranks of two variables; measures monotonic relationships even if non-linear. From −1 to 1.

**Seed** (*semilla*). Number that initialises the random generator. The same seed gives the same results.

**Sensitivity** (*sensibilidad*). How much a result changes when an input changes. Analysed with the tornado, spider chart and contribution to variance.

**Skewness** (*asimetría*). Measure of a distribution's lack of symmetry. Positive: long right tail (high values); negative: long left tail.

**Tax shield** (*escudo fiscal*). Tax savings from deductible expenses such as interest or depreciation.

**Tornado**. Sensitivity chart showing, from largest to smallest, how much the result changes when each variable moves between a low and a high value.

**Trial** (*prueba*, iteration). One simulation scenario: a draw of every assumption and a recalculation of the workbook.

**Truncation** (*truncamiento*). Restricting a distribution to an interval [minimum, maximum].

**Probability distribution** (*distribución de probabilidad*). Description of the values an uncertain variable can take and their probability. **Continuous** (any value in an interval) or **discrete** (separate values, like counts).

**VaR** (value at risk). Loss that won't be exceeded with a given probability; for a project, approximated by a low NPV percentile (P5 or P1).

**Variance** (*varianza*). Average squared deviation from the mean; the standard deviation is its square root.

**WACC** (weighted average cost of capital). Weighted average of the cost of equity and the after-tax cost of debt. The usual discount rate for the project cash flow.
